## Listing draft

Name candidate: Whiteboard

Short description:

Draw, write and organize ideas on an offline canvas. No account needed.

Full description draft:

Whiteboard gives your ideas room to grow. Sketch a plan, write study notes or arrange a diagram on a canvas you can move and zoom.

- Draw with a pen, marker or highlighter and choose color, width and opacity.
- Add shapes, arrows, text and sticky notes.
- Select, group, move, resize and duplicate objects, with Undo and Redo for document edits.
- Save boards on your device and return to them from your recent workspace.
- Organize boards in folders, search typed notes and page names, mark favorites, and restore boards from Trash.
- Start with editable brainstorming, Kanban, weekly-planning and study-note layouts.
- Add images and PDF pages; annotate them with drawing, text and notes.
- Export PNG/JPG images and multi-page PDFs or share through compatible apps on your device.
- Keep editable ZIP backups of saved boards and attachments; restore them as new copies.
- Choose a light, dark or system interface theme.

The core workspace works offline without registration. Boards stay in the application's local storage until you choose to export/share. Keep copies of important work: uninstalling the application removes local data. PNG exports are images, not editable document backups.

Only describe tested, actually shipped functions in the final listing. Do not advertise cloud, collaboration or AI before they are available. PDF export rasterizes imported PDF backgrounds; editable backups preserve the whiteboard document and source attachments.
